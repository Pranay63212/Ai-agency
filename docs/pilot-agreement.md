# Estimate Recovery Pilot Agreement (template)

> **Template only, not legal advice.** Have a lawyer in your state review this before you use it. Text in [brackets] is for you to fill in.

**Between:** [Your agency name] ("Agency") and [Contractor business name] ("Client")
**Date:** [date]

## 1. What the Agency will do
- Clean the Client's list of unsold estimates (up to **100 estimates**) and remove duplicates, invalid contacts and anyone already booked or who asked not to be contacted.
- Write and send a short follow-up sequence (about 3 messages over 10–14 days) by text and/or email **in the Client's business name**.
- Pass interested customers to the Client the same business day, and send a short results report at the end of the pilot.

## 2. What the Client will do
- Provide an export of unsold estimates: customer name, phone and/or email, job type, quote amount and quote date.
- **Confirm that every customer on the list gave the Client their contact details in connection with a quote**, and remove anyone who asked not to be contacted.
- Approve the message wording before anything is sent.
- Call or reply to interested customers promptly, and tell the Agency when a pilot customer books.

## 3. Pilot length and cost
- The pilot runs for **14 days** from the first message.
- **The pilot is free.** The Client owes nothing for it, including for jobs booked during it.

## 4. After the pilot (only if the Client chooses to continue)
- **Option A, pay per result:** $[200] per booked appointment from the follow-up campaign, invoiced monthly.
- **Option B, retainer:** $[750]/month for ongoing follow-up on every new unsold estimate.
- A job counts as "booked" when a customer contacted by the campaign schedules an appointment or accepts the quote within [60] days of their last campaign message. The Client reports bookings honestly; both parties can review the shared booking log at any time.

## 5. Consent and compliance
- Texts go only to customers who gave the Client their number. Every text includes opt-out instructions ("Reply STOP to opt out"), and opt-outs are honored immediately and permanently.
- Messages are sent only during normal hours (about 9am–8pm the customer's local time, Monday–Saturday).
- The Client is responsible for the accuracy of the contact list and for having the right to contact the people on it. [Have your lawyer review this allocation of responsibility, including any indemnity.]
- The Agency will register the texting number as required (for example A2P 10DLC) before sending texts.

## 6. Data
- The Agency uses the Client's customer data only for this campaign, keeps it private, and deletes it within [30] days of the Client's written request or the end of the engagement.
- The Agency never sells or shares the data.

## 7. Ending the agreement
Either side can end this agreement at any time with written notice (email is fine). Fees already earned for booked jobs stay payable.

## 8. No guarantee
The Agency cannot guarantee a particular number of bookings. The pilot is free so the Client can judge results before paying anything.
