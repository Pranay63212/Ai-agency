# Free Quote Follow-Up Pilot: Short Agreement

> Plain-language template, not legal advice. Fill in the [brackets]. For paid work later, use the longer `pilot-agreement.md` and have a lawyer look it over.

**Between** [Your name / agency] ("me") **and** [Company name] ("you"). **Date:** [date]

1. **What I'll do.** Follow up, in your company's name, with up to **100** customers from your list of old, unsold quotes, over about **14 days**. Anyone interested goes to you the same day. At the end, you get a short results report.
2. **What you'll do.** Send me the list (name, phone or email, job, amount, date). Approve the message wording before anything goes out. Contact interested customers quickly, and tell me when one books.
3. **Your customers' permission.** You confirm the people on the list gave you their contact details when they asked for a quote, and you'll remove anyone who asked not to be contacted. Every message includes a way to opt out, and opt-outs are honored right away.
4. **Cost.** **The pilot is free.** You owe nothing for it, including for jobs booked during it.
5. **After the pilot.** Only if you want to continue: $[200] per booked job from the follow-up, billed monthly. Nothing is owed unless you agree to continue in writing.
6. **Privacy.** I use your customer list only for this, never share or sell it, and delete it when you ask or when we stop working together.
7. **Ending it.** Either of us can stop at any time by email or text.
